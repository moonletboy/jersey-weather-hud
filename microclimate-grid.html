JERSEY MICROCLIMATE GRID

Jersey is only about 45 square miles, yet the weather can differ a lot between the sheltered south coast, the exposed Atlantic west and the high north coast. This page blends live readings from the personal weather stations across the island into one colour-contoured picture, so you can see where it is warmest, windiest or wettest right now.

PARISHES colours each parish by the average of the stations inside it. BANDS and SMOOTH estimate values between stations across the island (the nearest stations count most) and are a guide rather than a measurement. The dots are the stations themselves. Dashed lines are the parish borders, which run out to the low-water mark, so some shading sits over the tidal shore. Tap a dot or a parish for details.

Wind barbs: the staff points into the wind, each full feather is 10 knots, a half feather 5 knots and a flag 50 knots; a ring means calm. Barbs sway more on gusty stations and faster in stronger wind. The moving streaks show the wind flow estimated between stations.

AURORA OVAL · JERSEY

VISIBILITY CHECK

The aurora forms an oval-shaped ring around the magnetic pole. As geomagnetic activity (Kp) rises, that ring expands further from the pole and can push south enough to reach Jersey's latitude — 49.18°N. Because Jersey sits much further south than typical aurora-viewing latitudes, this only happens during severe-to-extreme geomagnetic storms (Kp 7–9). The estimate below is a rough guide based on Kp, not a precise real-time position.

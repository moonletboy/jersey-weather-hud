ABOUT THIS SITE & DISCLAIMER

About this site. Jersey Weather Chat is a community run by volunteers who love weather. This site pulls together public data and computer forecast models so islanders can see what’s coming and make their own decisions. It’s free, it always will be, and nobody’s paid to do it.

A few things to know before relying on it

  • Forecasts are predictions, not facts. They change with every update and are often wrong about timing and strength several days out. That’s why we show several models side by side — so you can see how much they agree before you decide.
  • This is not the official forecast or warning service. The authority for Jersey is Jersey Met (gov.je). Always check their warnings and follow official advice; if we and they disagree, trust Jersey Met.
  • We post about storms because the models show one — not to alarm anyone. We know Ciarán and Goretti left a mark. A storm that fizzles is good news, and we’ll say so. Please don’t shoot the messenger.
  • Use at your own risk. The site, its data and the Facebook group are provided “as is”. We can’t accept responsibility for decisions you make or any loss or damage arising from using them — boats, travel, outdoor work, events, property or anything else.
  • The data isn’t ours. It comes from NOAA, Open-Meteo, Jersey Met, USGS, EMSC, Weather Underground and others, and can be late, incomplete or wrong. The site itself may go down, usually at the worst moment.
  • Storm reports are from members and are not verified.
  • In an emergency call 999.

Every big weather model is run many times over with slightly different starting conditions, because nobody can measure today’s weather perfectly. This page takes every one of those runs for Jersey from four models — the European (ECMWF), American (GFS), German (ICON) and UK Met Office — and splits them into three scenarios — best case, most likely and worst case — each a range of gust speeds. A scenario’s chance is simply the share of runs that fall in it, so the three always add up to 100%. Pick a day below.

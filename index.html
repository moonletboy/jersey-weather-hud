Lifted Index · Atmospheric Stability
Compares a rising air parcel to its surroundings. Positive = stable air, storms unlikely. Negative = unstable air, storms more likely — the more negative, the greater the risk.

The temperature air must cool to for moisture to condense. Higher dew points mean muggier air and more fuel for storms — below 12°C is dry, above 18°C is humid/muggy.

COASTAL FLOOD RISK

HIGH TIDE OUTLOOK
LOADING HIGH TIDE OUTLOOK…
An indicative guide only — not an official warning. For each of the next four high tides it looks at two things. Flooding: the predicted high-water height plus the small lift from low air pressure (roughly 1cm per hPa below normal), onshore wind (south-easterly to south-westerly, which pushes water into St Aubin's Bay and St Clement) and wave height at that time — a big tide on its own is usually fine; the risk climbs when several of these line up. Waves over sea walls: in a storm, big waves and strong onshore gusts within two hours of high water can throw waves, spray and stones over sea walls and onto coast roads and promenades even on an ordinary tide. Tide height, wave height (out at sea and inshore) and gust strength are each scored, so very strong wind can make up for slightly smaller waves; the wind must be blowing onto the coast. Thresholds are approximate and for guidance. Always follow official Jersey Met and gov.je tide warnings, and call 999 in an emergency.
